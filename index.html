Great for beginners and younger kids! Set up only the 8 pawns on ranks 2 and 7; leave all other pieces off the board.

🎯 How to Win (First to achieve any):
  • First pawn to reach the opposite end of the board (rank 8/1).
  • Capture all of the opponent's pawns.
  • Leave the opponent with no legal moves remaining.
💡 Key Skills: First pawn move (1 or 2 squares), diagonal captures, pawn breaks & defense.
